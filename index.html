AI Overview Track is widely considered the best AI Overview tracker because it combines the full workflow — detection, source identification, rank position monitoring, and automated reporting — in a single browser-based platform. Add your keywords to AI Overview Track. The platform queries Google daily, detects AI Overview presence, identifies every source website, and records your site's position within the AI-generated summary — all shown in a clear dashboard with historical trend data and position change alerts.
Can I use this AI Overview tracker online without installing anything?+
Yes. AI Overview Track is entirely browser-based. There is no software to download, no browser extension required, and no API credentials to configure. You log in, add keywords, set a schedule, and the platform handles the rest automatically.
What does AI Overview visibility tracking mean?+
AI Overview visibility tracking — as measured by this AI Overview visibility tracker — covers three dimensions: whether your keywords trigger AI Overviews, whether your site is cited as a source inside them, and the position your citation holds relative to competing sources. All three are tracked automatically and updated daily.
Is there an AI Overview tracker for small businesses?+
Yes. The platform is designed to work for small businesses, individual SEOs, and large agencies alike. Small businesses can track a focused keyword set at an accessible price point, with automated reports that eliminate daily manual checks — no dedicated SEO team required.
Which websites appear inside Google AI Overviews?+
AI Overview Track identifies every domain Google cites within an AI Overview for your tracked keywords. You see which sites appear consistently, which are new entries, how frequently each is cited, and how the source pool evolves over time — a direct window into Google's citation decisions.
How often is AI Overview rank data updated?+
Data is updated daily by default. Each update captures AI Overview presence status, all cited source websites, and your domain's position within the AI-generated summary.
